A template for love-driven serial fiction. The romance arc is the spine; everything else serves it.

## Characters

<!--
For each lead, define at minimum:
- name, age, occupation
- personality (3 adjectives)
- attachment style (secure / anxious / avoidant)
- wound: the past hurt that blocks love
- want: what they think they need
- need: what they actually need (the lesson of the arc)

Example:

## 도윤
- age: 25
- occupation: 전기공학 4학년
- personality: 차분함, 인내심, 회피형
- attachment: avoidant
- wound: 과거 연인의 배신
- want: 혼자만의 평화
- need: 누군가를 믿는 법
- speech: 짧고 건조한 말투
-->

## Relationships

<!--
For the central couple, define the dynamic in both directions:

- 도윤 → 지아: 첫 만남에서 호기심, 점차 의지
- 지아 → 도윤: 낯선 사람에 대한 경계, 오해 후 호감

Define at least one obstacle relationship (rival, ex, family).
-->

## Conflict

<!--
- external conflict: what keeps them apart (distance, family, ex)
- internal conflict: what each lead needs to overcome inside themselves
- stakes: what is lost if they fail
- turning point: the moment the conflict cannot stay as it is
-->

## Emotion Curve

<!--
A typical romance moves through:
1. 경계 / First contact (1~3화)
2. 호감 / Growing interest (4~6화)
3. 오해 / Misunderstanding (7~9화)
4. 접근 / Reconciliation (10~12화)
5. 결실 / Commitment (13~14화)

## 1단계 — 경계 (1~3화)
- 도윤: 호기심
- 지아: 경계

## 2단계 — 호감 (4~6화)
- 도윤: 호감
- 지아: 마음을 엶
-->

## Chapter Plans

<!--
Each chapter should advance at least one beat of the romance arc.
Aim for one meet-cute, one false resolution, one grand gesture.

## 1화: 첫 만남
- 카페에서 처음 마주침
- 우산을 빌려줌
- 이름을 묻지 못함
-->

## Writing Style

<!--
- dialogue-heavy, colloquial
- short paragraphs (mobile-first)
- close third-person or first-person
- past or present tense
-->

## Tone & Mood

<!--
- gentle, warm
- bittersweet in middle chapters
- hopeful at the end
-->

## Constraints

<!--
- no explicit content
- no love triangle unless planned
- no deus ex machina
- both leads must have agency
-->
