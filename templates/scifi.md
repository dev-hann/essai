A template for science-fiction serials. The speculative idea and its consequences drive the plot.

## Characters

<!--
## Protagonist
- name, age, role
- background (origin, faction, training)
- expertise relevant to the speculative idea
- wound
- want vs need
- speech pattern

## Antagonist
- who they are
- what they want
- why they are not simply evil
-->

## Relationships

<!--
- protagonist ↔ antagonist
- protagonist ↔ allies
- human ↔ non-human / AI / alien
-->

## World

<!--
### Setting
- era (near-future / far-future / alternate)
- location (Earth / colony / ship / station)

### Society
- political system
- economy, class structure
- key institutions

### Technology Level
- what is everyday
- what is cutting-edge
- what is forbidden
-->

## Science / Speculative Idea

<!--
### The Premise
- the one speculative idea this story is built on
- e.g. memory can be transferred, FTL exists but ages the traveler, etc.

### Mechanism
- how it works (in-world, not real physics)
- what it requires (energy, cost, infrastructure)

### Limits
- what it cannot do
- what breaks if pushed

### Consequences
- social: who gains, who loses
- personal: what it costs the user
- second-order effects the plot will explore
-->

## Conflict

<!--
- idea-driven conflict (the premise creates the problem)
- character-driven conflict (internal cost of using the tech)
- stakes if unresolved
-->

## Emotion Curve

<!--
## 1단계 — 접촉 (1~3화)
- Protagonist: wonder, unease

## 2단계 — 몰입 (4~6화)
- Protagonist: commitment, discovery

## 3단계 — 위기 (7~9화)
- Protagonist: cost, doubt

## 4단계 — 선택 (10~12화)
- Protagonist: decision, sacrifice

## 5단계 — 여파 (13~14화)
- Protagonist: consequence, resolution
-->

## Chapter Plans

<!--
Each chapter should reveal one consequence of the speculative idea
OR advance one beat of the arc.

## 1화: 임계점
- 일상 속 기술 사용 장면

## Writing Style

<!--
- precise, concrete nouns
- explain the premise through use, not lecture
- avoid infodumps in narration
- one new concept per chapter maximum
-->

## Tone & Mood

<!--
- sense of wonder early
- dread as costs mount
- reflective at the end
-->

## Constraints

<!--
- no breaking the established speculative rules
- no hand-waving solutions
- side characters need motives
- technology must have a cost
-->
