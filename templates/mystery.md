A template for mystery serials. The puzzle and its fair-play clues are central.

## Characters

<!--
## Detective
- name, age, occupation
- method (deductive / intuitive / forensic)
- flaw that costs them
- wound
- speech pattern

## Victim
- who they were
- why someone wanted them dead
- secrets they were hiding

## Suspects
For each:
- name, relation to victim
- means, motive, opportunity
- what they are hiding (even if innocent)
- alibi
-->

## Relationships

<!--
- detective ↔ suspects: trust, suspicion, history
- suspects ↔ victim
- suspects ↔ each other
-->

## Mystery

<!--
### The Crime
- what happened
- when and where
- apparent vs actual cause

### The Truth
- who really did it
- how
- why

### The Red Herring
- the misleading clue / suspect
- why it misleads
- when it is cleared

### The Reveal
- when the detective solves it
- how the truth is shown to the reader
-->

## Clues

<!--
Fair-play clues the reader can use to solve it before the detective.

- clue 1 (chapter 2): physical evidence
- clue 2 (chapter 3): contradictory statement
- clue 3 (chapter 5): behavioral tell
- false clue (red herring)
- decisive clue (chapter N-1)
-->

## Timeline

<!--
- T-7 days: setup event
- T-1 day: last contact with victim
- T=0: crime
- T+1h: body discovered
- chapter-by-chapter investigation beats
-->

## Emotion Curve

<!--
## 1단계 — 발견 (1~2화)
- Detective: tension, curiosity

## 2단계 — 수사 (3~6화)
- Detective: obsession, doubt

## 3단계 — 교차 (7~9화)
- Detective: pressure, suspicion turns on them

## 4단계 — 해명 (10~12화)
- Detective: clarity, danger

## 5단계 — 귀결 (13~14화)
- Detective: resolution, cost
-->

## Chapter Plans

<!--
Each chapter should add at least one clue and at least one new suspicion.

## 1화: 발견
- 변호사의 시신 발견
- 형사 도착
- 첫 번째 단서
-->

## Writing Style

<!--
- tight, procedural
- concrete sensory detail at crime scenes
- dialogue carries suspicion
- avoid omniscient reveals
-->

## Tone & Mood

<!--
- unease throughout
- escalation of stakes
- cold clarity at the reveal
-->

## Constraints

<!--
- play fair: every decisive clue must appear before the reveal
- no revealing the killer's POV
- no last-chapter new characters
- no supernatural solutions in a non-supernatural mystery
-->
