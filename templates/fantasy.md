## Writing Style

<!--
- sensory, immersive
- concrete place details per scene
- avoid over-explaining lore in narration
- show the world through action
-->

## Tone & Mood

<!--
- wonder in early chapters
- dread in middle
- triumph tempered by loss at the end
-->

## Constraints

<!--
- no breaking established magic rules
- no unplanned new factions in late chapters
- side characters need motives
- no resolving climax via recap
-->
